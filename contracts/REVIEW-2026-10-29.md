## Findings

### F1 — Medium — a creator recipient can make `collect` revert for its coin (v3)
`_push` sends ETH with `to.call{value: amount}("")` and all remaining gas, and Solidity copies the
call's return data into memory even though it is discarded. A creator recipient that is a contract can
therefore (a) burn 63/64 of the gas, or (b) return a very large return-data blob ("return bomb"), so the
rest of `collect` runs out of gas and the whole call reverts. The refusal path (`owed`) never runs,
because the revert is of `collect` itself.

- Who is hurt: the protocol's 15–20% and a referrer's 5% for THAT coin stay in the pool, uncollectable
  while the recipient misbehaves. Traders are not affected; no other coin is affected.
- Who can do it: only the coin's current creator recipient — it would be withholding its own 80% too.
- (a) is surmountable with a high gas limit; (b) is not.
- Fix (needs a new factory): push ETH with a gas stipend and no return-data copy (assembly `call` with
  `returndatacopy` skipped), or credit `owed` and let recipients pull. Same pattern exists in v2.

### F2 — Low — the "seven-day stream" is a decay, not a schedule (RevenueShare)
Every arrival re-spreads ALL unreleased value over a fresh `STREAM` from now (`_syncView` sets
`finish = now + STREAM`). `collect` / `harvest` is public, so arrivals are frequent; anyone can also
send dust. The release then behaves like exponential decay with a ~7-day time constant (about 63%
released after 7 days without new arrivals, never "all of it in 7 days" while fees keep coming).
Nothing is lost and the anti-flash-stake property holds; the wording was wrong. **Fixed in the app**:
the coin page now says "each arrival is spread, with whatever is still unreleased, over the next seven
days". An auditor should confirm the README wording too.

### F3 — Low — `create` reverts when the same share already exists (RevenueShareDeployer)
`create` is open to anyone and the address is fixed by `(token, agent, agentBps)`, so a share with the
same parameters may already exist (made earlier, or by someone else — harmless, it is the same code
with the same immutables). `create` then reverts `AlreadyCreated`. **Fixed in the app**: it predicts
the address, checks for code, and skips `create` when the share is already there.

### F4 — Info — rounding dust stays in RevenueShare
`perShare += released * 1e36 / totalStaked` rounds down; the remainder is counted as released but paid
to no one, and is never re-accounted. At 1e36 scale this is dust per release. No fix needed.

### F5 — Info — a share's recipient switch is permanent by construction
`RevenueShare` has no function that calls `setCreatorRecipient`, so once a coin's recipient is set to a
share, the 80% can never be redirected. This is intended (it is what makes the share credible to
stakers). The app requires an explicit "cannot be undone" tick before signing.

### F6 — Info — stocks can be seized from a share
A Coinbase stock's issuer can move tokens. If the share's stock balance falls below `accounted`,
`_syncView` brings nothing in and payouts that can no longer be covered are refused and stay credited
(`Claimed(..., paid: false)`). Claims of the coin and ETH are unaffected.

## Checked and found as intended

- Reentrancy: every external entry point of `RevenueShare` and the fee paths of v3 are `nonReentrant`;
  pushes happen after state changes; a refused push restores the credit.
- Flash stake around `harvest`: an arrival is not paid at once; a stake earns only for the seconds it is
  staked, and an empty pool pauses the clock rather than releasing into nothing.
- Stake accounting by balance delta (`got`), so a token that delivers less than asked is counted as it
  arrived; the coin is a B20 with no admin, so its balance cannot be moved by anyone else.
- `claimable` runs the same `_syncView` on a copy, so what it shows is what a call would do.
- v3 split: creator `amount * 8000 / 10000`, referrer `amount * 500 / 10000`, protocol the remainder —
  measured on mainnet 2026-10-28 (tx `0x2d9b0407…16ac`): 80 / 5 / 15 to the wei.
- Referrers: only `approvedReferrers` at launch time count; unapproved ones emit `ReferralIgnored`.
